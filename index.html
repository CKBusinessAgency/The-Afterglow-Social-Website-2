About the evening

As the day winds down, the connections continue.

The Afterglow Social is a cocktail lounge experience in Houston during AfroTech week, bringing together professionals, entrepreneurs, creatives, and innovators for an evening of conversation, community, and curated experiences.

Designed to bring people together beyond the conference, The Afterglow Social offers a relaxed setting to unwind, meet new faces, exchange ideas, and build meaningful connections over cocktails, music, and good company.

Whether you're building a business, growing your career, exploring new ideas, or simply looking to connect with like-minded people, there's a place for you here.
